[Play Evo! Partner Expedition here](https://evo-expedition.pages.dev/). It is a card-based auto-battler built around team building and evolution.

Each round starts with preparation: recruit partners from the shop, arrange them in the front and back rows, or refresh the shop to look for a better fit. Three matching cards from the same family, star level, and branch can combine into an evolution. When your team is ready, start the battle and watch it play out automatically. Your decisions are who to bring, where to place them, and when to upgrade.

![The preparation screen, where you recruit partners and arrange your team](/images/games/evo-partner-expedition/preparation.png)

Along the way, you can encounter exploration events, equipment, and stronger opponents. After each fight, adjust your strategy for the next round. Here is a battle in progress.

![Partners fighting in an automatic battle](/images/games/evo-partner-expedition/battle.png)

If you want to try a quick run, start with the solo adventure. [Open the game and build your team](https://evo-expedition.pages.dev/).
